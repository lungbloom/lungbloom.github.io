Where is my data?

Only on your phone. There is no account, so deleting the app also deletes your progress.

I am not getting reminders

Allow notifications for Lungbloom in your phone settings and check the reminder time in the app's Settings tab.

I smoked. Do I lose everything?

No. Restarting the counter keeps your total savings, best run and badges.
