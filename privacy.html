Lungbloom Privacy Policy

Lungbloom has no user accounts and no server of its own.

Data stored on your phone

Your quit date, smoking habit, pack price, currency, reminder time, check-ins and progress are stored only on your device. We do not receive this data. It is removed when you use "Delete all data" in the app or delete the app.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone settings.

Advertising and tracking

This version of Lungbloom shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics services and does not track you. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Age

Lungbloom is intended for adults aged 18 or older.

Health information

Lungbloom is a motivation tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice.
